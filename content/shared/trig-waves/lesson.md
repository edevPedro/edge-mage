# Trig & waves (shared)

Sine and cosine are the language of **periodic signals**: amplitude, period \(T\), frequency \(f = 1/T\), and phase.

Edge sensors sample the world — Nyquist says you need more than \(2 f_{\max}\) samples/sec to avoid aliasing. Systems/signals rooms deepen FFT; Edge electric/robotics rooms use the same wave words.

`room_id: trig-waves` — single shared credit. Edge `trigonometria` / `ondas` rooms remain mastery / depth variants after this core.
